Strategy

A daily swing-trading scanner that hunts bullish continuation setups among the market's strongest movers.

Universe & screening

  • Pulls the top 200 tokens by market cap from CoinMarketCap, ranked by 24h % gain.
  • For each candidate, daily OHLCV candles are fetched from KuCoin.

Entry setup — all three must hold

  • C1 — Breakout: today's close is above yesterday's high.
  • C2 — Clean candle: a small upper wick (wick-to-body ratio < 0.25) — a strong body with little rejection.
  • C3 — Bearish prior candle (ideal): yesterday's candle was red/bearish.
  • C4 — Volume expansion: today's volume exceeds the average of the prior 5 days.

Trade levels (derived from the qualifying candle)

  • Entry = that candle's close
  • Stop Loss = that candle's low
  • Take Profit = Entry + (Entry − SL) × 2 → fixed 1:2 risk-reward

Exit rules (checked every day)

  • Stop Loss: if the day's low touches the SL → exit at SL (Loss).
  • Take Profit: if the day's high reaches the TP → exit at TP (Win).
  • Secondary exit: two consecutive red (bearish) daily closes → exit at the latest close (Win/Loss by actual PnL).
  • Peak gain since entry is tracked for reference.

Portfolio behavior

  • Tokens already open or already traded are skipped on new scans.
  • New qualifiers are logged as open positions; the dashboard tracks open positions, win rate, and profit factor over time.

Data sources: CoinMarketCap (top gainers list) · KuCoin (daily candles & price checks). Scanned automatically once per day via GitHub Actions.
